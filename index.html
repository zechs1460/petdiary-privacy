Pet Diary — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Publisher: JojoStudio Apps

Pet Diary (JojoStudio Apps) stores the pet records, photos, feeding/medication/health entries you create on your device. If you enable Cloud Backup, that data is synced to our backend (Supabase) tied to your anonymous or email account. We do not sell data, show ads, or collect analytics beyond crash-free operation. Purchases are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. You can export all your data anytime from Settings → Export My Data, and erase your account and all cloud data anytime from Settings → Delete account. Questions: jojos3dprints@gmail.com.

In short

  • Your diary lives on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on Cloud Backup.
  • Backup data is yours alone. Rows are stored per account and protected by row-level security, so only your own account can read them.
  • Photos stay on your device. Backup syncs the text and numbers of your entries, not the image files.
  • No ads. No analytics. No selling or sharing of your data.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat — we never see your card details.
  • Take your data with you: Settings → Export My Data produces a full JSON copy of your diary.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account removes your account and the records stored for it on our servers.
This page is the privacy policy for the Android app “Pet Diary: Vet & Med Tracker” (package com.jojostudio.petdiary) by JojoStudio Apps.
