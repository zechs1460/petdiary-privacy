Pet Diary — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Publisher: JojoStudio Apps

Pet Diary (JojoStudio Apps) stores the pet records, photos, feeding/medication/health entries you create on your device. If you enable Cloud Backup, that data is synced to our backend (Supabase) tied to your anonymous or email account. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. You can export all your data anytime from Settings → Export My Data, delete individual entries as you go, and erase everything from Settings → Delete account. Questions: jojos3dprints@gmail.com.

In short

  • Your diary lives on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on Cloud Backup.
  • Backup data is yours alone. Rows are stored per account and protected by row-level security, so only your own account can read them.
  • Photos stay on your device. Backup syncs the text and numbers of your entries, not the image files.
  • No ads, and no selling or sharing of your data. We collect no usage analytics, crash logs or diagnostics — the app ships without any analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • The one "Analytics" line in our Data safety form. Play shows Analytics against purchase history because the RevenueCat SDK reports purchase records, which is what makes your subscription and lifetime unlock work. It covers your purchase data only — never how you browse or use the app.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat — we never see your card details.
  • Take your data with you: Settings → Export My Data produces a full JSON copy of your diary.
  • Delete a single entry anytime: removing a pet, medication, feeding schedule, weight or symptom record deletes it from your device and marks it deleted in your backup, so it disappears from every device you sync.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account removes your account and deletes the records stored for it on our servers. One record is outside our reach — see What is deleted, and what is kept.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Individual entries. A record you delete is taken off your device right away. In your cloud backup the row is kept but stamped as deleted, because that is how backup avoids resurrecting something you removed on another device. Those stamped rows are readable only by your own account, and they are erased for good when you delete your account.

Deleting your account. Settings → Delete account performs a real deletion, not a deactivation. It removes your rows from every backup table along with your profile, then deletes the account itself. Nothing is kept and there is no waiting period.

One record we cannot delete for you. RevenueCat keeps its own customer and purchase record so your subscription stays verifiable across reinstalls, and the in-app deletion does not reach it. To have that removed as well, email jojos3dprints@gmail.com and we will delete your RevenueCat customer record on request. Removing it does not cancel anything billed by Google Play — cancel a subscription in Play itself.
